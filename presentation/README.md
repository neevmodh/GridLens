[Live application](https://gridlens-50047133296.development.catalystappsail.in/)

The PowerPoint includes editable text, diagrams and a native data chart. Speaker notes contain sources and explanation. Cover artwork depicts a conceptual microgrid. Product images show the actual deployed application. Images and individual slide previews are included in the adjacent folders.

QuickML metrics come from its built-in evaluation and do not represent hidden-test accuracy. The original evaluator checks prediction shape/count/finiteness. Battery charge and switching commands are simulations, with no physical hardware connected.
